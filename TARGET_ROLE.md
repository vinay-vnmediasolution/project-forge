# Target Role

**Purpose:** Build toward Forward Deployed Engineer–grade capability — the
intersection of production engineering, applied AI, and customer-facing
delivery. This file defines what the market screens for, so the programme is
driven by requirements rather than assumption.

This file is public deliberately. The scores below are honest rather than
flattering, and they are expected to move in both directions.

---

## What the role is

**Titles:** Forward Deployed Engineer / Forward Deployed Software Engineer.
The same work is hired under plainer names: Applied AI Engineer, AI Solutions
Engineer, Customer/Deployment Engineer, Technical Deployment Lead, Solutions
Architect (AI).

**The shape of it:** embed with a customer, understand the real problem
quickly, design and build the solution, deploy it, and prove it created value.
Palantir describes the responsibilities as similar to working in a small startup: small teams, broad technical work and end-to-end ownership.

Every capability on this list compounds into independent consulting delivery.
Nothing here is single-purpose.

## Consolidated requirements

Working synthesis from the verified postings above and previously reviewed role material. Company-specific claims remain attributable; recurring patterns are treated as hypotheses until the source set is refreshed.

### R1 — Production engineering experience
Hands-on engineering or technical deployment experience, typically customer-facing, with the ability to write, review and debug production-grade code. Current OpenAI and Salesforce roles explicitly require end-to-end delivery and code ownership; Palantir lists proficiency in languages such as Python, Java, C++ or TypeScript.

### R2 — LLM and agentic systems fluency
Hands-on experience building or deploying LLM-powered systems in client or production environments, including understanding how model behaviour affects product experience. Current OpenAI and Salesforce deployment roles ask for shipped AI/LLM experience rather than theoretical familiarity alone.

### R3 — Evaluation-driven thinking
Systematic evaluation using representative data, production signals and human judgement. Some published accounts characterise evals as non-negotiable in frontier-lab hiring; use that characterisation only with the specific account attached. The directly verifiable claim is narrower: current OpenAI deployment roles explicitly mention evals, error analysis and tightening success criteria over time.

### R4 — Data engineering at scale
Data modelling, integration and the ability to work with complex or messy data recur in the verified postings. Specific tools such as Spark, Airflow or dbt are useful only when supported by a target posting or the active build; they are not universal FDE requirements.

### R5 — Full-stack breadth
Debug across data, backend and application layers, and carry a system from prototype through deployment and operation. Cloud, API, integration, reliability and observability depth recur, but the exact stack varies by employer.

### R6 — Rapid prototyping under ambiguity
Prototype from a business requirement, iterate on real-world feedback, build
from scratch when needed. Palantir describes projects starting from open-ended
questions. Explicitly: strong problem-solving in ambiguous environments.

### R7 — Customer-facing ownership
Lead technical discussions with stakeholders from engineers to executives;
translate business priorities into delivery. Gather requirements, analyse
workflows, define a roadmap from prototype to scaled deployment.

### R8 — Regulated-environment delivery
Regulated-environment experience is relevant where customer work involves government, financial services or healthcare. The verified Palantir Australian role is government-focused and clearance-gated; Salesforce lists regulated-industry experience as preferred rather than universal.

### R9 — Enterprise systems literacy
Familiarity with enterprise or operational systems — ERPs, CRMs, ITSM, GIS.

### R10 — Disposition
Ownership, customer empathy, problem decomposition, product sense and willingness to travel. Travel varies by employer and role: current postings range from “as needed” to 25–50%. Interview-format claims require a dated candidate or employer source before public reuse.

## Gap scoring

Scored personally, not by a model. Against a hiring panel's standard, not a
peer group's.

- **1** — no evidence I could show anyone
- **2** — conceptual understanding, nothing built
- **3** — built it once, could not defend it under questioning
- **4** — shipped it, can explain the trade-offs, have been wrong and corrected
- **5** — could teach it, and a third party has relied on it
