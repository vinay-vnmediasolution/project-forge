## Progression method
- Primary target: finish most working sets with **1–2 RIR** and clean technique.
- 3+ RIR: consider a small increase when safe.
- 1–2 RIR: correct training load.
- 0 RIR: do not automatically increase; consolidate the load and improve reps/form.
- Increase only after the target sets and reps are repeatable with good technique.
- Separate working-weight benchmarks from top-set PBs.
