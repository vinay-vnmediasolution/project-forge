## Progression rules
- Most working sets should finish with 1–2 RIR and clean technique.
- At 3+ RIR, consider a small increase if technique is stable.
- At 0 RIR, do not automatically increase; repeat and consolidate.
- Progress by adding reps first, then load, unless the initial load is clearly too light.
- Keep top-set PBs separate from normal working weights.
- Avoid testing failure on compound lifts every session.
