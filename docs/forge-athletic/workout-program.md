# Project Athletic 80 — Rolling Workout Program

_Last updated: 6 August 2026_

## Operating model
This program is not tied to weekdays.

**Primary cycle:**

Push A → Legs A → Pull A → Recovery as needed → Push B → Legs B → Pull B → Repeat

A missed session is not skipped permanently. Continue with the next session in the rolling sequence. An optional Arms + Core session may be inserted when a lower-body session needs to be postponed, but it does not replace the next programmed leg session.

## Global rules
- Main compound lifts: usually 2–3 minutes rest.
- Secondary compound lifts: 90–120 seconds rest.
- Isolation and core work: 45–75 seconds rest.
- Target most working sets at 1–2 RIR.
- Keep Smith squat, free-bar squat and leg-press benchmarks separate.
- Use weights below as starting prescriptions, then adjust from first-set RIR and technique.

---

## Push A — Dumbbell hypertrophy emphasis

1. **Flat dumbbell bench press**
   - Warm-up: 20 kg each × 12
   - 27.5 kg each × 10
   - 30 kg each × 8–10
   - 30 kg each × 8–10
   - Rest: 2 minutes

2. **Incline dumbbell press**
   - 25–30 kg each × 8–10 × 3
   - Use 30 kg only when recovered; current best is 30 kg × 8 × 3
   - Rest: 90–120 seconds

3. **Seated dumbbell shoulder press**
   - 20–22.5 kg each × 8–10 × 2–3
   - Use a neutral grip if more comfortable
   - Do not push through elbow pain
   - Rest: 90 seconds

4. **Dumbbell lateral raise**
   - 8 kg each × 10 × 3
   - Build toward 12 × 3 before increasing
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

5. **Rope triceps pushdown**
   - 25 kg × 8–10 × 3
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

6. **Overhead cable triceps extension**
   - 21.25 kg × 8–10 × 3
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

---

## Legs A — Free-bar strength emphasis

1. **Free-bar back squat**
   - Warm-up: empty bar × 10; 40 kg × 8; 60 kg × 5
   - Working target: 70 kg total × 6 × 3–4
   - Current benchmark: 70 kg × 6 × 4
   - Rest: 2–3 minutes

2. **Romanian deadlift**
   - 27.5 kg dumbbells each × 8–10 × 3
   - Rest: 90–120 seconds

3. **Leg press**
   - 70 kg per side × 10 warm-up/entry set
   - 80 kg per side × 8–10 × 2
   - Build toward 80 kg per side × 10 × 3
   - Rest: 90–120 seconds

4. **Leg extension**
   - 23 kg × 12 × 3
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

5. **Seated calf raise**
   - 51.3 kg effective resistance × 10–12 × 3
   - Rest: 45–60 seconds

6. **Hanging knee raise**
   - 15 × 3
   - Rest: 45–60 seconds

---

## Pull A — Vertical pull and unilateral row emphasis

1. **Pull-ups**
   - Bodyweight × 6 × 4
   - Progress one set at a time toward 4 × 7
   - Rest: 2 minutes

2. **Single-arm dumbbell row**
   - 32.5 kg each × 8–10 × 3 as next progression
   - Fall back to 30 kg × 10 if form becomes unstable
   - Rest: 90 seconds

3. **Lat pulldown**
   - 60 kg × 10
   - 70 kg × 8
   - 70 kg × 8–10
   - Rest: 75–90 seconds

4. **Seated cable row**
   - 60 kg × 10
   - 65 kg × 8–10
   - 65 kg × 8–10
   - Rest: 75–90 seconds

5. **EZ-bar curl**
   - 30 kg × 8 × 3
   - Build toward 3 × 10
   - Rest: 75–90 seconds

6. **Hammer curl**
   - 15 kg each × 8 × 3
   - Build toward 3 × 10
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

## Push B — Barbell strength plus chest volume

1. **Flat barbell bench press**
   - Warm-up: empty bar; 40 kg × 8; 60 kg × 5–8
   - Working volume: 70 kg × 6–8 × 2–3
   - Optional top set only when recovered and technically sound: 75–80 kg × 4–6
   - Current top-set PB: 80 kg × 6 @ 0 RIR
   - Avoid testing failure every session
   - Rest: 2–3 minutes

2. **Incline dumbbell press**
   - 30 kg each × 8 × 3
   - Repeat until sets finish with 1–2 RIR before increasing
   - Rest: 90–120 seconds

3. **Machine chest press**
   - 60 kg × 6–8 × 3
   - Rest: 75–90 seconds

4. **Dumbbell lateral raise**
   - 8 kg each × 10 × 3
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

5. **Rope triceps pushdown**
   - 25 kg × 8–10 × 3
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

6. **Overhead cable triceps extension**
   - 21.25 kg × 8–10 × 3
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

---

## Legs B — Smith, posterior chain and machine emphasis

1. **Smith-machine squat**
   - Log plates per side; do not combine with free-bar PBs
   - Warm up progressively
   - 30 kg per side × 8 × 2–3
   - Current baseline: 30 kg per side × 8 × 2
   - Rest: 2–3 minutes

2. **Dumbbell Romanian deadlift**
   - 27.5 kg each × 8–10 × 3
   - Rest: 90–120 seconds

3. **Leg press**
   - 70 kg per side × 10 entry set
   - 80 kg per side × 8–10 × 2
   - Current top set: 80 kg per side × 10 @ 1–2 RIR
   - Rest: 90–120 seconds

4. **Leg curl**
   - 41 kg × 10 × 3
   - Current session result: 36 × 12, 41 × 10, 41 × 10
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

5. **Standing Smith calf raise**
   - 20 kg per side × 12 × 3
   - Full stretch, pause at top, no bouncing
   - Rest: 45–60 seconds

6. **Hanging knee raise**
   - 15 × 3
   - Rest: 45–60 seconds

---

## Pull B — Horizontal row and rear-delt emphasis

1. **Pull-ups**
   - Bodyweight × 6–7 × 4
   - Rest: 2 minutes

2. **Chest-supported row**
   - 70 kg × 8–10 × 3
   - Historical result: 70 kg × 10, 10, 8
   - Rest: 90 seconds

3. **Wide- or neutral-grip lat pulldown**
   - 65–70 kg × 8–10 × 3
   - Rest: 75–90 seconds

4. **Cable row variation**
   - 60–65 kg × 8–10 × 3
   - Rest: 75–90 seconds

5. **Rear-delt fly machine**
   - 45 kg × 10 × 3
   - Rest: 60–75 seconds

6. **Biceps choice**
   - EZ-bar curl 30 kg × 8–10 × 2–3, or
   - Hammer curl 15 kg each × 8–10 × 2–3
   - Reduce direct arm volume if the optional Arms + Core session was performed recently

## Optional Arms + Core session
Use only as a floating session; it does not replace the main PPL cycle.

1. EZ-bar curl — 30 kg × 8 × 3
2. Hammer curl — 15 kg each × 8 × 3
3. Rope triceps pushdown — 25 kg × 8–10 × 3
4. Overhead cable extension — 21.25 kg × 8–10 × 3
5. Rear-delt fly machine — 45 kg × 10 × 3
6. Hanging knee raise — 15 × 3
7. Optional plank — 60 seconds × 2–3

## Recovery decision
Take a recovery day when soreness, sleep, motivation or performance suggests accumulated fatigue. Recovery target: easy walking, 8,000–12,000 steps, 10–15 minutes mobility, normal protein and daily creatine.
